## Medallion / architecture-layer descriptions (for Reference Architecture section)

**Raw (Bronze) layer:** stores a one-to-one copy of source data in its original, unprocessed form with basic quality gates — a single repository for all data, supporting structured, semi-structured, and unstructured formats. Validation is limited to basic quality & completeness checks.

**Integrated (Silver) layer:** the curated layer where data is cleaned, transformed, and enriched for consistency, accuracy, and completeness — used for exploration, preparation, and data-quality analysis. More structured than Bronze; the data model is designed business-use-case-agnostic.

**Use-case (Gold) layer:** the trusted, ready-to-consume layer where models are prepared for business needs and decision-making. Highly structured, consistent, reliable — the single source of truth for the organization.

**Data delivery layer:** provides logic and processes user requests — APIs, web services, downstream systems, visualization, microservices.

**Job Orchestration:** managing and coordinating execution of data-processing jobs — scheduling, monitoring, dependency management, error/exception handling.

**Traceability & reconciliation:** data lineage and audit trails (where data came from, who accessed it, what processing steps, where stored) plus reconciliation comparing datasets to ensure consistency and accuracy.

**Constraint checks:** automated validation that data meets defined rules and relationships.

**Runtime Audits:** checks during processing to ensure jobs run efficiently and produce accurate results; surface bottlenecks and resource issues.

**Housekeeping:** archiving and purging to keep the lake clean, efficient, performant, and cost-optimized.
